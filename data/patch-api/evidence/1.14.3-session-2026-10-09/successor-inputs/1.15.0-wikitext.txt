==Summary==
* This patch incorporates all API changes from [[World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Classic|Wrath Classic]] in [[Patch 3.4.3/API changes|Patch 3.4.3]].
** A subset of Dragonflight API changes from [[Patch 10.1.7/API changes|Patch 10.1.7]] through to [[Patch 10.2.0/API changes|Patch 10.2.0]] are also included.
** Refer to the linked articles for guidance on any impactful changes.
* Added a new C_Engraving namespace for managing runes in seasonal content.
