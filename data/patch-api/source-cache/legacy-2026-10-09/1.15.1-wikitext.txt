* A subset of Dragonflight API changes from [[Patch 10.2.5/API changes|Patch 10.2.5]] are included.

** A deprecation alias is included that allows usages of <code>Enum.SeasonID.Placeholder</code> to continue working as-is for detecting Season of Discovery realms.
