## Ranked opportunities (no tests deleted)

Costs below are summed per-test process wall seconds, including contention, not CPU seconds or promised suite wall savings. Four-worker division is only an optimistic planning estimate; exclusive gates and timeout-child slots invalidate simple linear scaling.

1. **Repeated full-startup publication fixtures: migrate, not delete.** All 22 `tests/patch_*_publication_sweep.rs` cases call the identical `common/publication_sweep.rs` preload. 2. **Startup/load-error overlap: deletion review, not automatic equivalence.** Most ordinary `*_loads_without_errors` cases already moved to prefork (2031 cases total). Removing these fork-only probes cannot save repeated startup; unmeasured savings, likely small. `addon_coverage_lod` is 668.812s/24 cases but tests explicit LoD loading and prior-shard state: do not delete as startup duplicates.
